Sync from Zoho

Reads the real departments, teams, agents and the Classification picklist from Zoho Desk, so mappings below are made from live values rather than guesses.

Employees

Employees appear here automatically the first time they sign in with Zoho. Pre-creating a row is optional. A row must be connected before that person can raise a ticket — that is what makes their CRM notes genuinely theirs.

Teams

The Zoho Desk team id decides where the ticket is escalated. A team with no id cannot be used.

Classifications

Each classification routes to a team and picks a template. Desk value is the exact string stored in the Zoho Desk Classification field — leave it empty and the field is simply not set. Needs review blocks the classification from being used at all.

Ticket templates

The ticket body is rendered from these on the server. Available tokens: {azeerName} {credUser} {credPass} {businessId} {waba} {platformName} {crmUrl} {descriptionEn} {shortTitle} {expectedResult} {frequency} {stepsToReproduce} {troubleshooting} {evidence} {frUseCase} {frRequestedFeature} {frExpectedBenefit} {frPriority}

Troubleshooting cases

Each case is a branching flow. Every answer carries the Arabic label the employee reads, the professional English sentence recorded on the ticket, and either the next step or a final outcome.

Ticket submissions

Every Create Ticket attempt, whether or not it produced a ticket.
